EASY PASS WEBSITE
=================

index.html   The whole website. The pictures, design (CSS) and code (JS)
             are all built INSIDE this one file, so it works on its own —
             just double-click it to open in Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
thanks.html  Page students see after sending the form (also self-contained).

mask-color.png, flyer.jpg, favicon.png
             Spare copies of the pictures for your use (social media,
             printing). The website does NOT need them to run.

IMPORTANT: unzip first ("Extract all"), then open index.html.
Opening it from inside the zip or from a WhatsApp preview can show
a blank or broken page.

PUT IT ONLINE (free)
  Netlify: go to app.netlify.com/drop and drag the unzipped folder in.
  Then add your own domain under Domain settings. Replace
  "easypass.com.gh" in index.html with your real domain.
