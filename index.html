        Questions students ask

        How much does it cost?

        Prices depend on the service, level, length and deadline. Send your request and we'll give you a clear quote before any work starts. Part payments are accepted for long projects.

        How do I pay?

        Mobile Money (MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash, AirtelTigo Money) or bank transfer. We confirm every payment on WhatsApp.

        Will you follow my school's format?

        Yes. Upload your institution's template or project guidelines in the form and we'll follow its structure, fonts, margins and referencing style.

        Is my information kept private?

        Always. Your name, student ID and files are only used for your request and are never shared with anyone.

        Can I ask for corrections?

        Yes. Supervisor comments and corrections on work we've done together are handled until your submission.

        Do laptops come with a warranty?

        Yes. New and refurbished laptops come with a warranty, and we'll install Windows, Office and your course software free on purchase.
